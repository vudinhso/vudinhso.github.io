Dinh-Son has obtained his PhD in Mechanical Engineering and Robotics at Laval University, Canada, which focused on the design and control of a mechanism for lower limb rehabilitation.
His postdoctoral fellowship focused on the design and control of exoskeletons for home use, performed in Aalborg University, Denmark.
Dinh-Son has taught three years as an Assistant Professor in AUM, Kuwait in Mechatronics. 
He is continuing his career in RMIT, Vietnam as a Lecturer in Robotics and Mechatronics department.
His research interests focus on exoskeletons, parallel robots, and human-robot interaction.
